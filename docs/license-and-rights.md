## Known rights scope

- **Known font notices:** the bundled Bricolage Grotesque and Fraunces font files carry the SIL Open Font License (OFL), Version 1.1, with their notices in `apps/worker/assets/nuglet-short/`. Those font terms do not license the rest of this repository. See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- **Product-owned Nuglet scope:** Nuglet lesson content, recipes, generation instructions, brand presentation, logo, end cards, and product-specific media are eligible for inclusion because Nuglet information may be public. Their ownership, contributor permissions, and redistribution terms still need to be recorded before publication.
- **Repository code and general documentation:** project-owned code and general documentation are covered by MIT. A contributor keeps responsibility for confirming that submitted material may be shared under those terms.
- **Generated output and source material:** rights depend on the source, provider terms, prompt, and destination. Each package should carry usage-rights metadata, but metadata does not replace a rights review.

## Unresolved decisions

The owner must explicitly decide and document:

1. whether project-owned Nuglet recipes, content, logo, hero/end-card art, and media will be included under MIT or separately licensed;
2. who owns or controls the logo and hero assets and whether redistribution is allowed;
3. whether the DOCX and source materials under `docs/` may be redistributed;
4. whether generated audio, images, video, and other media have rights suitable for redistribution;
5. how third-party notices and attribution must ship with any distribution; and
6. maintainer ownership and authority to approve a release and accept contributions.

Until the remaining rights and ownership decisions are recorded, do not describe the repository as ready for public publication or publish a release artifact.

## Contribution rights

A pull request is a proposed change, not a separate license grant. Contributors should submit only material they have the right to share and should identify third-party or product-owned material. Maintainers must resolve the contribution and ownership policy before accepting contributions into a distributed release.

See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the known font notices and remaining asset review work.

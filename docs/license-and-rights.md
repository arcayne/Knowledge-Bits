# License and rights

**No license has been selected or granted for this repository.** Do not treat the absence of a `LICENSE` file as permission to copy, modify, redistribute, publish, or commercially use repository code, documentation, media, or brand assets. The owner must choose and record a license before a public release or license grant.

This page records open decisions. It is not a license and it is not a rights grant.

## Known rights scope

- **Known font notices:** the bundled Bricolage Grotesque and Fraunces font files carry the SIL Open Font License (OFL), Version 1.1, with their notices in `apps/worker/assets/nuglet-short/`. Those font terms do not license the rest of this repository. See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- **Product-owned Nuglet scope:** Nuglet lesson content, recipes, generation instructions, brand presentation, logo, end cards, and product-specific media are product-owned or product-supplied materials in this repository's current workflow. Their ownership, contributor permissions, and redistribution terms have not been fully documented for an open-source release.
- **Repository code and documentation:** the owner has not selected a license or contribution copyright policy. A contributor must not assume that a pull request grants broad downstream rights.
- **Generated output and source material:** rights depend on the source, provider terms, prompt, and destination. Each package should carry usage-rights metadata, but metadata does not replace a rights review.

## Unresolved decisions

The owner must explicitly decide and document:

1. whether the code and general documentation will receive an open-source license;
2. whether product-owned Nuglet recipes, content, logo, hero/end-card art, and media will be included, excluded, or separately licensed;
3. who owns or controls the logo and hero assets and whether redistribution is allowed;
4. whether the DOCX and source materials under `docs/` may be redistributed;
5. whether generated audio, images, video, and other media have rights suitable for redistribution;
6. how third-party notices and attribution must ship with any distribution; and
7. maintainer ownership and authority to approve a release and accept contributions.

Until these decisions are recorded, do not add a `LICENSE` file, do not describe the repository as open source, and do not publish a release artifact.

## Contribution rights

A pull request is a proposed change, not a license grant. Until the owner selects a license and contribution terms, contributors should submit only material they have the right to share and should identify third-party or product-owned material. Maintainers must resolve the contribution and ownership policy before accepting contributions into a licensed distribution.

## Known rights scope

- **Known font notices:** the bundled Bricolage Grotesque and Fraunces font files carry the SIL Open Font License (OFL), Version 1.1, with their notices in `apps/worker/assets/nuglet-short/`. Those font terms do not license the rest of this repository. See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- **Product-owned Nuglet scope:** Nuglet lesson content, recipes, generation instructions, brand presentation, logo, end cards, and product-specific media are eligible for inclusion because Nuglet information may be public. Their candidate disposition is recorded in [Public release rights inventory](release-rights.md); trademark rights remain reserved.
- **Repository code and general documentation:** project-owned code and general documentation are covered by MIT. A contributor keeps responsibility for confirming that submitted material may be shared under those terms.
- **Generated output and source material:** rights depend on the source, provider terms, prompt, and destination. Each package should carry usage-rights metadata, but metadata does not replace a rights review.

## Ongoing rights controls

The release owner must keep these controls documented:

1. keep third-party notices beside bundled fonts and future external assets;
2. record owner, source, permission, and license for each new binary or source snapshot;
3. keep Nuglet trademarks separate from the MIT software grant;
4. exclude generated media until provider and source rights are recorded; and
5. keep the maintainer and release authority current in `SECURITY.md` and `docs/release-rights.md`.

## Contribution rights

A pull request is a proposed change, not a separate license grant. Contributors should submit only material they have the right to share and should identify third-party or product-owned material. Maintainers must check the rights inventory before accepting a binary, source snapshot, prompt corpus, recipe, or media file.

See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the known font notices and remaining asset review work.
